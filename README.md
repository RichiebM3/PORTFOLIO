## 🚀 Featured Project: Autonomous Ferry Charging System

### **Washington State Department of Transportation Collaboration**

Developed an innovative charging device for autonomous water ferries, harnessing environmental energy for sustainable transportation.

**Key Achievements:**
- ⚡ Engineered complete solar power management system
- 🧲 Designed Faraday system with neodymium magnets and coiled copper wire
- 🔋 Integrated dual 5V solar panels with two rechargeable 12V lithium batteries
- 🌡️ Implemented temperature monitoring and safety systems
- 🔧 Added Zener diodes for voltage stability
- 💻 Developed custom microcontroller code for energy management
- 📡 Enabled seamless Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity
- 🎨 Designed 3D model in Blender and fabricated with 3D printer

**Technologies Used:**
- Arduino Uno R4
- C/C++ (Microcontroller Programming)
- Solar Power Management
- Battery Management Systems
- Wireless Communication (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth)
- 3D Modeling & Printing

**Impact:**  
Successfully powered an autonomous ferry buoy, demonstrating practical application of renewable energy in maritime transportation infrastructure.
